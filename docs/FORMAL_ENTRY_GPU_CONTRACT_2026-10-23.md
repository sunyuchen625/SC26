## Timing contract: established differences

The public CPU driver measures with `MPI_Wtime()` in seconds but labels the mean `ms`. The public GPU driver explicitly converts to milliseconds. The team's research driver correctly labels its prototype mean in seconds. The actual scoring parser/version must settle which line and unit it consumes.

The public GPU driver recreates the adapter in each of five trials. It measures upload and solution download separately. Its local scored interval is:

```text
(create and status handling)
+ (solve callback and status handling)
+ (post-download status/metrics/destruction)
```

Specifically, it sums `(upload_start-start) + (solve_stop-solve_start) + (stop-copy_stop)`, converts to milliseconds, averages five trials locally, then takes the MPI maximum. It excludes the two transfer calls and also omits the post-upload status/barrier gap. Therefore its value is not simply full wall time minus two transfer numbers. Upload/copy callbacks must complete asynchronous transfers before returning. An MPI status reduction synchronizes ranks, not GPU streams. The solver must finish counted device work before `solve` returns, using the library's demonstrated completion contract or an appropriate device fence; pending compute must not spill into excluded solution download. Device/runtime initialization belongs at the accepted counted lifecycle point. The actual synchronization and metrics behavior must be checked in the selected harness.

The current **3.903–3.907 s** result includes its own assembly, migration, setup, solve, diagnostics and cleanup. Preparation probes are host-observed call intervals; they cannot be subtracted to manufacture a formal transfer-excluded score. GPU memory phase observations likewise do not replace the selected API's tracked-allocation and observed-delta semantics. The public metrics are themselves sampled. T2 has no problem-specific memory cap, so these fields concern truthful reporting and accepted interface semantics; the actual job grant still constrains resource use.

The public rules exclude format conversion and sorting. Placement of CSR/IJ construction and internal staging must follow the accepted entry. Numerical AMG/preconditioner setup remains counted; it must not be moved into an excluded transfer callback. Structural counts remain value-independent, and five full lifecycles remain the accepted research baseline pending the actual test contract.
